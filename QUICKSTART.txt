SNAP VANISH - QUICK START (no admin rights, nothing to install)
================================================================

1. Run the app
   Double-click SnapVanish.exe in this folder.
   If Windows SmartScreen warns you: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

2. Add the Meet camera to Chrome or Edge (one time only)
   - Chrome: open chrome://extensions    Edge: open edge://extensions
   - Turn on "Developer mode" (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge)
   - Click "Load unpacked" and select the "browser_extension" folder
     that sits next to SnapVanish.exe.
   - Keep that folder where it is; the browser loads it from there.

3. Start the effect
   - In Snap Vanish, choose your webcam and press Start.
   - Press "Capture clean background (3 s)" and step out of the frame.

4. Join Google Meet
   - Open meet.google.com (reload the tab if it was already open).
   - Settings -> Video -> Camera -> "Snap Vanish Camera".
   - Snap your fingers to vanish, snap again to reappear
     (or press Space / "Trigger now" in the app).

Keep Snap Vanish running during the call. It owns your real webcam and
Meet gets the processed video from it. All video stays on this computer;
the app only listens on 127.0.0.1.
